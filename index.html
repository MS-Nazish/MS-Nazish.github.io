01 · About Me

Turning ideas into reliable digital products

I'm Nazish Khan, a BS Information Technology graduate of Government College University Hyderabad (GCUH) and a Full-Stack & AI Developer.

I build complete products — the interface, the API, the database and the deployment. My recent work includes SiteSync, a MERN + PWA attendance and payroll system for construction contractors with GPS-verified attendance and offline mode, and SchoolHub, a React Native school management app for admins, teachers and parents backed by Firebase.

I care about what clients care about: clear communication, honest timelines, clean code and features that solve real problems — including Urdu / right-to-left support for local users. I keep learning constantly, with 30 certificates from Google (via Coursera) and the DigiSkills.pk program in AI, data analytics, freelancing and more.
